Why surf camp

The ocean is for everybody.

Resonance Surf Camp is a program of The Peyton Project, a Knoxville 501(c)(3). It started with Peyton, who didn't speak, and for years nobody could hear what he had to say, until a letter board changed everything.

Camp carries that idea to the beach for every kind of ability: wheelchair users, kids who are blind or Deaf, autistic and non-speaking kids, and everyone in between. We meet each surfer where they are, presume competence, and make the ocean a place of joy.

Questions families ask

My child has never been in the ocean. Is that okay?

Yes. Many surfers start on the sand and only go as far as they're comfortable. There's no pressure to stand up, or to go in at all.

My child uses a wheelchair. Can they surf?

CONFIRM Yes. Instructors help with transfers, and adaptive boards let surfers ride seated or lying down.

Does my surfer need to know how to swim?

CONFIRM POLICY Every surfer wears a life vest and stays with instructors in the water.

Can parents surf too?

Yes. Parents stay on the beach all day, and they're welcome to get in the water.

Can siblings come too?

Yes, siblings are welcome.

What does it cost?

Camp is free. Families only cover their own travel and lodging.
